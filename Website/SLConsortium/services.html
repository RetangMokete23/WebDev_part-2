Our Services

  • Debt Counselling

    A full assessment of your financial situation to determine whether you are over-indebted, and guidance on the best course of action available to you under the National Credit Act.

  • Debt Review

    We manage the formal debt review application process on your behalf — negotiating with credit providers, restructuring repayment plans, and applying to court where required — so that you have one affordable, legally protected monthly payment.

  • Financial Consulting and Budgeting Advice

    Practical, ongoing budgeting support to help clients build sustainable financial habits during and after the debt review process.

How the Debt Review Process Works

  1. Initial consultation and full financial assessment.
  2. Formal application for debt review with the National Credit Regulator.
  3. Negotiation with credit providers to restructure repayments.
  4. Court order confirming the new, affordable repayment plan (where required).
  5. Ongoing monthly payment through a registered payment distribution agent until you are debt-free.
