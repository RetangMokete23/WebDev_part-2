About SL Consortium

Our History

SL Consortium is a registered debt counselling and financial consulting business operating in Limpopo, South Africa. The company was established to guide over-indebted consumers through the formal debt review process introduced under the National Credit Act 34 of 2005, offering an ethical, regulated alternative to informal or predatory lenders.

Our Mission

To help financially over-indebted individuals regain control of their finances through professional, ethical, and NCR-compliant debt counselling and financial consulting services.

Our Vision

To be the most trusted and accessible debt counselling and financial consulting firm in Limpopo, recognised for transparency, compliance, and measurable client outcomes.

NCR Registration

SL Consortium is registered with the National Credit Regulator (NCR) as a debt counsellor under registration number NCRDC4427. This registration confirms that SL Consortium is legally authorised to assess consumers' financial circumstances and apply for debt review on their behalf.
